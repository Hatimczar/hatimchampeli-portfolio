If you're in the UAE and weighing whether to upgrade this cycle, the number that actually matters isn't the US price everyone quotes, it's the AED figure that shows up at checkout on the UAE Apple Store. Here's what UAE outlets are reporting, and one way to soften the increase.

## What UAE buyers are looking at

For context, the current iPhone 17 Pro starts at **Dh4,699** for 256GB in the UAE. Analysts covering the region expect a real jump this cycle:

- **iPhone 18 Pro**: estimated to start around **Dh5,099**, with configurations reported up to **Dh5,999**
- **iPhone 18 Pro Max**: estimated to start around **Dh5,549**
- **iPhone Ultra (foldable)**: entry-level estimated between **Dh5,950 and Dh6,600**, with higher-storage configurations reported to climb **well above Dh10,000**

None of these are official. Apple hasn't announced UAE pricing as of writing, and analysts are working from component-cost estimates and a rumored $100 to $300 US price increase, not a confirmed price sheet. Treat every AED figure circulating before Apple's own UAE store listing goes live as a planning estimate, not a quote.

## The timeline that matters more than the price

UAE outlets are consistently reporting the UAE as one of the first markets globally to get this year's lineup:

- Event: September 9, 9 p.m. Dubai time
- Pre-orders: expected to open September 12
- In stores: September 18

That's a genuinely fast turnaround for the region relative to past cycles, worth knowing if you're planning to line up for launch day rather than wait.

## Offsetting the increase: Apple's UAE trade-in program

If the estimated price jump is giving you pause, Apple's Trade In program in the UAE currently accepts **iPhone 13 and newer**, with credit of up to **Dh2,220** for a qualifying device in good condition. That's not a guaranteed number for whatever you're trading in, and Apple hasn't published trade-in values specific to the iPhone 18 Pro yet, but it's a real lever worth using rather than paying full price outright.

Trade-in value depends on the exact model, storage size, and physical condition, screen cracks, button function, and battery health all factor in, and Apple only confirms the final figure after inspecting the device. If you're planning to trade in, back up your data, sign out of your Apple Account, and check Apple's current terms before you hand the phone over.

## The practical read

If you're on an iPhone 15 or older, the jump to iPhone 18 Pro is going to be a genuinely bigger number this year than last cycle's update was. If you have an eligible trade-in sitting in a drawer, checking its estimated value before September 18 is worth the five minutes, since that's the difference between the sticker estimate above and what you'll actually pay.
